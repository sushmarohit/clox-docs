## Tagline

---

## Company summary

CLOX is positioned as Australia's smartest way to ship freight — bringing simplicity, transparency, and visibility to the supply chain. The platform replaces high-overhead manual freight brokerage with a sovereign digital ecosystem that connects corporate senders directly with vetted heavy-vehicle operators, securing transactions via upfront escrow protection.

> Smart freight forwarding made simple, transparent, and efficient. No complicated forms or endless phone calls — just what you need to move goods quickly, affordably, and securely.

---

## Product positioning

- **Category:** Next-generation autonomous 4PL logistics
- **Geography:** Australia-first; architecture supports future expansion
- **Value proposition:** Bridge shippers seeking competitive pricing with carriers optimizing asset utilization; remove frictional broker costs

---

## Customer value pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Instant comparisons | Live choices from vetted providers; compare ETA, ratings, and price |
| Streamlined booking | Digitized workflow; book in a few clicks |
| Real-time tracking | Live cargo updates from dispatch to delivery |
| Escrow protection | Upfront payment security; carrier paid on successful delivery |

---

## Four-step sender journey

1. **Enter details** — Cargo requirements and destinations
2. **Compare** — Pricing and reliability from vetted carriers
3. **Book** — Secure shipment with confidence
4. **Track** — Live monitoring to final arrival

---

## Ecosystem segments

### Shippers

Enterprise and commercial businesses: transparent pricing, reliable provider comparison, optimized logistics spend.

### Carriers

Transport operators and fleets: fill capacity, expand network, guaranteed fast payment via secure upfront escrow.
