## Context

In addition to the general duty **not to drive a fatigue-regulated heavy vehicle while fatigued**, drivers must comply with maximum work and minimum rest limits.

Parties in the supply chain must do everything reasonably practicable to prevent drivers from exceeding these limits (similar to OHS laws). Drivers must be allowed to stop if at risk of exceeding limits and make alternative arrangements.

## Standard hours

Standard hours apply to all drivers who do **not** have fatigue management accreditation.

### Solo drivers

| In any period of… | Max work | Min rest |
|-------------------|----------|----------|
| 5½ hours | 5¼ hours work time | 15 continuous minutes rest time |
| 8 hours | 7½ hours work time | 30 minutes rest in blocks of 15 continuous minutes |
| 11 hours | 10 hours work time | 60 minutes rest in blocks of 15 continuous minutes |
| 24 hours | 12 hours work time | 7 continuous hours stationary rest time* |
| 7 days | 72 hours work time | 24 continuous hours stationary rest time |
| 14 days | 144 hours work time | 2× night rest breaks and 2× night rest breaks taken on consecutive days |

\* **Stationary rest time** = time out of a heavy vehicle or in an approved sleeper berth of a **stationary** heavy vehicle.

**Night rest breaks** = 7 continuous hours stationary rest between **10pm and 8am** (driver base timezone) **OR** 24 continuous hours stationary rest.

### Solo drivers — bus and coach sector only

| In any period of… | Max work | Min rest |
|-------------------|----------|----------|
| 5½ hours | 5¼ hours work time | 15 continuous minutes rest |
| 8 hours | 7½ hours work time | 30 minutes rest in 15-min blocks |
| 11 hours | 10 hours work time | 60 minutes rest in 15-min blocks |
| 24 hours | 12 hours work time | 7 continuous hours stationary rest* |
| 7 days | — | 6× night rest breaks |
| 28 days | 288 hours work time | 4× 24 continuous hours stationary rest |

### Two-up drivers (Standard)

| In any period of… | Max work | Min rest |
|-------------------|----------|----------|
| 5½ hours | 5¼ hours work time | 15 continuous minutes rest |
| 8 hours | 7½ hours work time | 30 minutes rest in 15-min blocks |
| 11 hours | 10 hours work time | 60 minutes rest in 15-min blocks |
| 24 hours | 12 hours work time | 5 continuous hours stationary rest* **OR** 5 hours continuous rest in approved sleeper berth while vehicle is moving |
| 52 hours | — | 10 continuous hours stationary rest time |
| 7 days | 60 hours work time | 24 continuous hours stationary rest **and** 24 hours stationary rest in blocks of ≥ 7 continuous hours |
| 14 days | 120 hours work time | 2× night rest breaks and 2× night rest breaks on consecutive days |

**Sleeper berth (Two-up):** NHVR recommends using sleeper berth for continuous rest (vehicle moving) only **prior to** a driver’s major rest break.

### Swapping from AFM to another work and rest option

Drivers changing from AFM to Standard Hours (or BFM) must **not** drive a fatigue-regulated HV unless:

1. They comply with all work and rest requirements of the **new** option, **and**  
2. They have had **48 continuous hours** of rest.

## Basic Fatigue Management (BFM)

BFM is a module of the **National Heavy Vehicle Accreditation Scheme (NHVAS)**. Operators with BFM accreditation get more flexible hours (among other things, work up to **14 hours** in a 24-hour period) if fatigue risks are properly managed.

### BFM Solo drivers

| In any period of… | Max work | Min rest |
|-------------------|----------|----------|
| 6¼ hours | 6 hours work time | 15 continuous minutes rest |
| 9 hours | 8½ hours work time | 30 minutes rest in 15-min blocks |
| 12 hours | 11 hours work time | 60 minutes rest in 15-min blocks |
| 24 hours | 14 hours work time | 7 continuous hours stationary rest* |
| 7 days | 36 hours long/night work time** | No limit has been set |
| 14 days | 144 hours work time | 24 continuous hours stationary rest after no more than 84 hours work time; **and** 24 continuous hours stationary rest; **and** 2× night rest breaks; **and** 2× night rest breaks on consecutive days |

\* Stationary rest = out of regulated HV or approved sleeper berth of stationary regulated HV.

\*\* **Long/night work time** = any work time (outside midnight–6:00 am) exceeding 12 hours of work in a 24-hour period, **or** any work time between midnight and 6 am (or equivalent in driver base timezone).

### BFM Two-up drivers

| In any period of… | Max work | Min rest |
|-------------------|----------|----------|
| 24 hours | 14 hours work time | No limit has been set |
| 82 hours | No limit has been set | 10 continuous hours stationary rest* |
| 7 days | 70 hours work time | 24 continuous hours stationary rest **and** 24 hours stationary rest in blocks of ≥ 7 continuous hours |
| 14 days | 140 hours work time | 4 night rest breaks |

### Swapping between BFM and Standard Hours

Drivers changing from BFM to Standard Hours must **not** drive a fatigue-regulated HV unless:

1. They comply with all Standard Hours requirements, **or**  
2. They have had **48 continuous hours** of rest.

## Advanced Fatigue Management (AFM)

AFM is currently the only HVNL mechanism for **tailored** work and rest hours. See NHVR Advanced Fatigue Management guidance for operator accreditation details.

**Clox Phase 1:** AFM automation is **out of scope**.

## Personal use of a fatigue-regulated heavy vehicle

Any activity involving a fatigue-regulated HV counts as **work time** under HVNL. Drivers may need personal use when another means of transport is not reasonably available.

**National Heavy Vehicle Work and Rest Hours Exemption (Personal Use) Notice 2025 (No.1)** provides up to **one hour** additional work time for a **permitted personal activity**.

### Permitted personal activity

Work for personal, **non-commercial** purposes, including:

- Stowing or retrieving personal effects from the vehicle  
- Driving for personal reasons (e.g. groceries, bank, post office)

May be used:

- During the driver’s **24 continuous-hour** stationary rest break (Standard, BFM, AFM)  
- Any time where all exemption conditions are met (Standard Hours)

**Cannot** be used for commercial pressure (e.g. driving to a mechanic for repairs, or getting home at end of a commercial journey).

See NHVR *Guideline for the National Personal Use Work and Rest Hours Exemption*.
