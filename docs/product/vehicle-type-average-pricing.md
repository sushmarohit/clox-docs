## Purpose

Realistic Australian market average pricing ranges for hourly and per-kilometre rates. Suitable for **vehicle selection, quoting, matching, and carrier validation** — **guidance, not fixed quotes**. Prices vary by city, fuel, demand, and access conditions. Rates are **per vehicle, not per pallet**.

## 1. Light Vehicles FLV (Local / Express)

### Motorbike / Scooter

- Payload: up to **30 kg**
- Per hour: **$45 – $70**
- Per km: **$1.20 – $1.80**
- Volume: small satchel
- Use: documents, urgent parcels

### Car / Hatchback / Sedan

- Payload: **100 – 200 kg**
- Per hour: **$50 – $80**
- Per km: **$1.40 – $2.00**
- Volume: ~0.5 m³
- Use: small parcels, urgent deliveries

### Ute (Single Cab) & (Dual Cab)

- Payload: **800 – 1,000 kg**
- Per hour: **$70 – $100**
- Per km: **$2.00 – $3.00**
- Tray: ~2.4 m × 1.8 m
- Use: building materials, pallets, machinery

## Sender vs Carrier Vehicle Selection Flow

### Core principle

- **Sender** defines the freight requirement  
- **Carrier** confirms the vehicle that meets it  
- Keeps liability clear and reduces disputes  

### Sender flow (primary control)

#### Step 1 — Enter shipment details (cannot skip)

- Pickup & delivery locations  
- Freight type (cartons / pallets / machinery / loose)  
- Weight (kg)  
- Dimensions (L × W × H)  
- Pallet count  
- Special requirements: ☐ Tailgate · ☐ Forklift on site · ☐ Refrigerated · ☐ Crane (Hiab) · ☐ Time-critical  

#### Step 2 — System vehicle recommendation (auto)

- Calculates minimum required vehicle  
- Shows recommended types (e.g. “Large Van or 3-Tonne Truck”)  
- Undersized vehicles **disabled**  
- Larger vehicles allowed (higher cost)  

#### Step 3 — Sender vehicle preference (optional)

- Select preferred type (e.g. “3-Tonne with Tailgate”) **OR** “Any suitable vehicle”  
- Sender selects **requirements**, not a specific truck or driver  

#### Step 4 — Base rate visibility

Sender sees price ranges, ETA, and capacity indicators:

| Indicator | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 🟢 Perfect fit | Suitable |
| 🟡 Extra space | Larger OK |
| 🔴 Risk of overload | **Blocked** |

### Carrier flow (confirmation & responsibility)

#### Step 5 — Carrier views job requirements

Freight specs, required vehicle class, mandatory features, sender preference (if any).

#### Step 6 — Carrier submits proposal

Must select actual fleet vehicle (type, capacity, rego, features) and assign driver + licence class.

**System validation:** blocks undersized vehicles; blocks licence mismatch.

#### Step 7 — Smart conflict handling

| Case | Result |
|------|--------|
| Larger vehicle | Allowed (price adjusts) |
| Smaller vehicle | **Blocked** |
| Same truck on multiple jobs | On award → other bids **auto-expire**; carrier notified |

#### Step 8 — Job award & lock

When sender accepts:

- Vehicle + driver **locked**  
- Other proposals auto-cancelled  
- Job status → Confirmed  

**Liability:**

- Carrier responsible for confirmed driver and vehicle  
- Sender responsible for correct freight details, locations, booking date/time  

### Exception handling

#### Vehicle breakdown

- Carrier requests substitution; replacement must meet or exceed original capacity  
- Instant notification  
- Penalty charge may apply per hour on base rate  

#### Freight mismatch at pickup

- Heavier/larger than declared → carrier can reject **or** request upgrade via app  
- App logs evidence (photos + timestamp)  

### Why this flow works

- Prevents under-quoting  
- Stops wrong vehicle bookings  
- Clear liability  
- Scales for enterprise  
- Matches how real transport businesses operate

## Truck Breakdown — App Screen Flow

### 1. Driver App

| Screen | State / Content |
|--------|-----------------|
| **1 Active Trip** | Map, ETA, Trip ID, Pickup→Drop; Complete Trip (disabled); **Report Issue** |
| **2 Report Issue** | Radio: Truck Breakdown · Accident · Traffic Delay · Other → Continue |
| **3 Breakdown Details** | Type dropdown (Engine, Tyre, Electrical, Accident-related, Other); auto GPS; optional photo + note → Submit |
| **4 Submitted** | Status `Breakdown – In Progress` (penalty start time); breakdown time/location; message that Sender, Receiver, Clox Admin, Transport admin auto-notified; Update Repair Status · Contact Company · Emergency Call (optional) |

### 2. Transport Company App / Web

| Screen | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **5 Alert** | “Truck KA01AB1234 reported breakdown on Trip #CLOX-9087” → View Trip |
| **6 Management** | Trip summary (driver, vehicle, location, goods) |

**Resolution options:**

| Option | Actions |
|--------|---------|
| **A Assign Replacement** | Select vehicle + driver + ETA → Propose Replacement |
| **B Repair & Continue** | Enter repair ETA → Update Repair ETA |
| **C Cancel Trip** | Reason mandatory; penalty warning → Cancel Trip |

### 3. Sender App

| Screen | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **7 Notification** | “Your shipment has encountered a breakdown. Action required.” |
| **8 Details** | Reason, map, time, status; if replacement: new vehicle/driver/ETA → Approve / Reject |
| **9 Outcome** | Approved → Trip Resumed + live tracking; Rejected → TCO + Admin alerted; possible cancel flow |

### 4. Admin panel

| Screen | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **10 Monitoring** | Table: Trip ID, Company, Breakdown type, Delay, Resolution; Force replacement/cancel; Waive/apply penalties; Admin notes |
| **11 Completion** | Final ETA; delay tagged Breakdown; fare adjusted if applicable; logged to Company Performance Score + Driver history |

## CLOX — Load Types (Customer Selection)

### General Freight (default)

Cartons · Palletised goods · Retail stock · FMCG

### Temperature Controlled

Chilled · Frozen · Perishable food · Pharmaceuticals  
🔒 Requires refrigerated vehicle

### Dangerous Goods (DG)

Flammable liquids/solids · Corrosives · Toxics · Aerosols · Batteries · Gas cylinders · Chemicals  
Requires DG-licensed carrier; correct placards & paperwork

### Liquid / Bulk Liquid

Drums · IBCs · Tanker · Oils · Paints · Food-grade liquids  
May require spill containment, DG placard paperwork, food-grade certification

### Heavy Freight

Machinery · Engines · Industrial/mining equipment (weight-critical; higher-capacity vehicles)

### Oversize / Over-dimension

Long steel · Pipes · Beams · Structural steel · Precast · Large equipment  
May require flatbed/extendable trailer, permits, pilot/escort

### Steel & Metal

Coils · Plates · Rebar · Aluminium · Sheets  
Requires chains & restraints; NHVR load restraint compliance

### Timber & Building Materials

Timber packs · Plywood · Gyprock · Bricks · Roofing

### Plant & Vehicles

Forklifts · Excavators · Skid steers · Cars · Tractors · Agricultural equipment  
Often requires ramps or low loader

### Bulk / Loose Load

Sand · Gravel · Soil · Scrap metal · Waste materials  
Tip truck or walking floor

### Containers (Port / Wharf)

20ft · 40ft · Empty · Reefer  
Wharf access & time slots apply

### Furniture & Household

Commercial furniture · Office fit-outs · Large items (non-removalist)

### High-Value / Sensitive

Electronics · Medical equipment · Fragile machinery · Controlled goods

### Waste / Recycling

Industrial · Medical · Construction waste · Recyclables  
EPA compliance may apply
